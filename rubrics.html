CREASEIQ COACHING STANDARD

Coaching rubrics.

Standards for batting, pace bowling, and spin bowling.

Batting rubrics

Exemplary: balanced, controlled execution. Competent: a strong foundation with small refinements. Needs practice: a fault that risks control, timing or dismissal.

Cover drive

Positive stride, head over front foot, high elbow, straight bat, late contact and balanced finish.

Drop-ball drive through target gate.

Straight / off drive

Head over ball, front foot to line, stable base, high elbow and full face along ground.

Ground-drive target gate.

On-drive

Head and front shoulder lead, top-hand control, vertical path and anchored back toe.

Half-volleys through mid-on cones.

Back-foot punch

Back-and-across movement, head over back foot, high elbow and downward punch.

Short-hop deflection.

Square / late cut

Deep back-foot movement, arm extension, horizontal arc and wrist roll.

Wide off-stump feeds.

Pull / hook

Head inside line, stable base, horizontal swing and eyes on ball; hook requires body inside bouncer line.

Soft-ball pulls and bouncer tracking.

Forward / backward defensive

Move fully to line, vertical bat, head over relevant foot, bat close to pad, soft hands.

Block feeds inside two-metre circle.

Leave

Off-stump judgment, bat withdrawn early, hands close and eyes through to keeper.

Call Play or Leave before bounce.

Leg glance / flick

Create room, keep face straight until impact and use a late wrist deflection.

Stump-line feeds past fine leg.

Sweep

Front foot down line, low one-knee base, head over knee and low flat swing.

Paddle-sweep progression.

Pace-bowling rubrics

Outswinger

Seam to slips; moves away; best with a new shiny ball.

Inswinger

Seam toward fine leg; moves into pads; targets stumps and LBW.

Straight / wobble seam

Upright seam creates sharp nip; wobble seam creates irregular movement.

Reverse swing

Uses rough side; creates late opposite swing with an older ball.

Side-on / front-on / semi-open

Consistent lower-body and shoulder alignment: square, open, or 45°.

Mixed action — injury risk

Side-on lower body with forced front-on shoulder twist can stress the lower back.

Release phase

Extended arm near ear, firm wrist behind upright seam, front arm down and braced front knee.

Spin-bowling rubrics

Finger spin

Off-break: off-to-leg. Left-arm orthodox: leg-to-off. Doosra: reverse off-break.

Finger-spin variations

Carrom: thumb-and-ring-finger flick. Arm ball: follows arm. Knuckle/top spinner: dip and bounce.

Wrist spin

Leg-break: leg-to-off. Googly: disguised off-to-leg turn.

Wrist-spin variations

Flipper skids low; slider has backspin and little turn; top-spinner creates dip and bounce.
